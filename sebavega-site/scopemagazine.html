Overview

The brief

Bring different editorial voices into one recognizable magazine. Recommendations, criticism, profiles, and interviews need distinct reading rhythms without feeling like unrelated publications.

The concept

Treat anime as a broad visual culture. Use a recognizable masthead, modular layouts, and recurring orange accents while allowing each story to establish its own atmosphere.

  2. 2 Explorations

    Design for different reading speeds.

    Product-led departments use open space and concise details. Features use strong openings and multi-column stories. Profiles introduce their subjects through portraits and expressive display type.

  3. 3 Typography & color

    Build hierarchy without flattening the stories.

    Bold display type introduces stories while columns organize longer text. White pages support editorial reading; dark pages create a cinematic shift. Orange recurs across titles, dividers, and callouts.
