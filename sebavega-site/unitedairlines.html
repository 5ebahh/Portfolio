Context

The problem

Our interface review and interview synthesis pointed to crowded layouts and difficulty finding key tasks. For occasional travelers, earning points did not always translate into understanding a useful benefit.

My goal

Make reward options easier to discover and understand, while giving booking, trips, and support clear places in the app. The aim was clearer decisions, not simply more engagement.

My contribution

I owned the rewards redesign, assisted teammates, and reviewed their designs for consistency. Booking, boarding, trip management, and support are team work.

From question to direction

The process

The observations, decisions, and open questions behind the concept.

Research

Look for friction around real travel tasks.

The team combined interface and competitive reviews with interviews, affinity mapping, personas, and journey maps. Rewards emerged as a comprehension problem: travelers needed to see what points could mean for their next trip.

[placeholder: add interview sample, recruitment criteria, and one traceable finding]

Direction

Give each task a predictable home.

The proposed architecture separates Home, Bookings, My Trips, Rewards, and Profile. Giving rewards its own destination creates room to explain benefits without crowding the day-of-travel experience.

My design

Put potential value beside the balance.

I redesigned rewards around the points balance, reward options, missions, and cards. The detail concept shows proposed upgrade and gifting actions. Eligibility, pricing, and benefits are concept content that would need business validation.

Iteration & testing

Test comprehension before adding complexity.

The presentation contains final concepts but no documented usability-test results. A next study would ask travelers to explain an available benefit, identify restrictions, and find a redemption path. Missions should earn their place by helping rather than distracting.

The design direction

Final design

The final team concept covers booking, trips, boarding, rewards, and support. My rewards work makes available options more visible and gives each benefit a place for explanation.

Show the next useful action

Pair the balance with options rather than making points the end of the journey.

Outcome & reflection

What was delivered

Delivered research synthesis, a revised architecture, and mobile concepts. No launch, usability improvement, or retention increase is claimed.

What comes next

Next, I would test reward eligibility and redemption comprehension with frequent and occasional travelers, then use the observed errors to refine the hierarchy.
