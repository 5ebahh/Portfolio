Overview

The problem

Interview synthesis pointed to cluttered discovery, uncertainty about reviews, and a desire for recommendations from trusted people. Finding a business was only part of the decision; people also needed context for choosing it.

My goal

Connect discovery to community while giving search, maps, messages, and saved places a clear structure. A social layer should support the choice without competing with it.

My contribution

I served as project lead. The research and mobile concept are team work. [add describe the activities and screens I personally owned]

Final design

The high-fidelity concept brings discovery, community, and personal content into a connected experience. Search results help people compare; messages and highlights add social context; saved collections support a return visit.

Outcome & reflection

What was delivered

The team delivered research themes, personas, journey maps, revised navigation, mid-fidelity flows, and a high-fidelity concept. Faster discovery and increased review confidence remain unverified.

What comes next

Next, I would test whether people can compare suitable places and find messages or saved collections without help. Social context alone does not establish review credibility.
