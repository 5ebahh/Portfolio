Cinnamon Toast Punk

Solo ARTD 251 project · Special-edition concept

A familiar cereal. A louder personality.

A punk-inspired reimagining of Cinnamon Toast Crunch, from expressive packaging to a spray-can collectible. The cereal stays recognizable while the attitude changes.

Context

The brief

Reimagine an existing cereal as a special edition for younger audiences and collectors. The package needed a distinctive personality while keeping a clear connection to the original product.

The concept

Translate the brand’s chaotic energy into graffiti lettering, punk-styled Cinnamojis, paint splatters, and a controlled blue, pink, and purple palette.

My contribution

I developed the concept, visual identity, illustrations, box design, dieline, mockups, and collectible direction.

From question to direction

The process

Sketches, explorations, and the choices that hold the work together.

Sketches

Start with attitude, then find structure.

The project developed from an initial sketch through multiple drafts. The central idea was a rebellious street-inspired edition that still reads as a cereal package.

Explorations

Let feedback sharpen the direction.

Feedback called for more focused graffiti references, a stronger cereal cutout, a believable brick setting, and a tighter palette. Those comments helped move the package from general visual noise toward a clearer concept.

Typography & color

Keep the chaos intentional.

A graffiti title leads the front panel. Blue, pink, and purple unify the splatters and characters, while the cereal opening preserves a product cue.

Refinement

Think beyond the front panel.

The design extends across the dieline with nutrition information and character details. Spray-can keychains carry the same visual language into blue, pink, purple, and a rare chromatic concept.

The design direction

Final design

The special edition combines a graffiti identity, wall-shaped cereal window, expressive characters, and a coordinated collectible extension.

Keep recognition

Retain the Cinnamojis and a visible cereal cue.

Extend one concept

Carry lettering, color, and attitude from box to collectible.
