Overview

The brief

Explore the relationship between Bauhaus and AI through a sustainable-living concept. The brief raises a tension between streamlined form and the human character that efficiency can leave behind.

The concept

Use a repeated halo to connect structure, silhouette, and storytelling. Botanical colors and warm materials balance geometric precision. Sustainability is a design theme, not a verified product performance claim.

Process

Sketches, explorations, and the choices that hold the work together.

  1. 1 Sketches

    Find a form that can travel.

    Initial sketches explored cylindrical and circular forms across a mat, lamp, and table set. Repetition offered a way to make different objects feel related.

  2. 2 Explorations

    Build from structure, not a style shortcut.

    The Wagenfeld lamp informed geometric clarity; Breuer’s B9 tubular-steel tables informed light structural loops. The halo became a shared element across the collection.

  3. 3 Typography & color

    Let the palette carry warmth.

    Green, terracotta, brown, and cream support the botanical direction. Chrome-like steel, walnut, and woven fiber are proposed material cues in the concept imagery.

Final design

Three speculative objects share a circular language without becoming identical: a patterned mat, an orb light held in a loop, and a coordinated table-and-stool set.
